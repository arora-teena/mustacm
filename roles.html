Roles & Responsibilities

👩‍💻 Member Roles & Responsibilities

As a member of the ACM-W Student Chapter, Mody University, you play an important role in shaping the chapter’s growth and impact. Every member is encouraged to actively contribute and uphold the values of ACM-W.

🌟 General Responsibilities

  • Actively participate in ACM-W events, workshops, and meetings
  • Maintain professionalism and follow the ACM-W Code of Conduct
  • Represent the chapter positively within and outside the university
  • Respect diversity, inclusivity, and teamwork
  • Share ideas, feedback, and suggestions for improvement

📚 Learning & Skill Development

  • Engage in technical and non-technical learning opportunities
  • Take initiative to learn new technologies and skills
  • Participate in hackathons, coding events, and speaker sessions
  • Support peer learning and knowledge sharing

🤝 Collaboration & Teamwork

  • Work collaboratively with fellow members and the core team
  • Volunteer for organizing events and activities
  • Assist in planning, execution, and promotion of events
  • Communicate clearly and responsibly within teams

📣 Communication & Outreach

  • Stay active on official communication channels
  • Help promote ACM-W events on social media and within campus
  • Encourage participation from other students
  • Maintain respectful and clear communication at all times

💡 Initiative & Leadership

  • Take ownership of tasks assigned by the core team
  • Show leadership qualities when opportunities arise
  • Contribute innovative ideas for events, collaborations, and initiatives
  • Support the mission of empowering women in computing

🏆 Professional Conduct

  • Maintain punctuality and discipline during events and meetings
  • Complete assigned tasks within deadlines
  • Uphold the reputation and ethics of ACM and ACM-W
  • Follow university rules and chapter guidelines
