Code of Conduct

Code of Conduct Guidelines

  • Membership in MUST ACM-W is voluntary.
  • Members must maintain respectful and inclusive behavior.
  • Participation in meetings and activities is mandatory.
  • No sharing of confidential ACM-W information.
  • Academic integrity and ethical behavior are mandatory.
  • Harassment of any form is strictly prohibited.
  • Members must uphold the reputation of MUST ACM-W.
  • Violation may result in suspension or removal.

Declaration

I confirm that I have read, understood, and voluntarily agree to follow the Code of Conduct and represent MUST ACM-W Student Chapter responsibly.
