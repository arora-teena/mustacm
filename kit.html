🤝 ACM-W Code of Conduct

  • Maintain a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment
  • Treat everyone with dignity, fairness, and respect
  • Encourage diversity of thoughts, backgrounds, and skills
  • No tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate behavior
  • Maintain integrity in academic, technical, and organizational activities
  • Follow university and ACM ethical guidelines at all times
  • Violation of the code may lead to disciplinary action as per chapter policies

📅 Event Participation Guidelines

  • Prior registration is mandatory for all events
  • Be punctual and maintain discipline during sessions
  • Active participation is encouraged during discussions and activities
  • Certificates (if applicable) will be provided based on attendance and engagement
  • Follow event-specific rules shared by the organizing team
  • Members are encouraged to volunteer and contribute to event organization

💬 Communication Channels

Official communication will be shared through:

  • 📢 WhatsApp Groups – Updates, reminders, and quick announcements
  • 📧 Email – Formal communication, invitations, and certificates
  • 📸 Instagram & LinkedIn – Event highlights, opportunities, and achievements
  • 🗂️ Google Drive / Forms – Registrations, resources, and feedback

Members are requested to regularly check these channels to stay updated.
